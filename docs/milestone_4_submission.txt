1. What I Built & How It Works:
For this milestone, I designed and deployed a Kimball star schema warehouse in PostgreSQL under the analytics schema. It organizes raw agricultural price records into clean dimensions and a central fact table:

• dim_date: Tracks 249 monthly periods (2006 to 2024) with calendar year, month, quarter, and Kenya crop season flags (Long Rains harvest, Short Rains harvest, and off-season).
• dim_commodity: Tracks 51 distinct food items with CPI staple indicators and food categories (cereals, pulses, vegetables, meat).
• dim_location: Tracks 226 market locations mapped across Kenya's 47 counties and GPS coordinates.
• fact_food_prices: Stores 28,463 price observation rows with foreign keys to all dimensions, standardized price per KG/L, raw price, and change percentages.
• Idempotent Loading: Uses a UNIQUE constraint on (date_id, commodity_id, location_id, price_type) with ON CONFLICT DO UPDATE to ensure zero duplicate records on repeated ingestion runs.

3. How I Tested It (Analytical Queries):

Query 1: Wholesale vs. Retail Price Gap for White Maize in 2020:
SQL:
SELECT d.year, f.price_type, ROUND(AVG(f.price_per_kg)::numeric, 2) AS avg_price, COUNT(*) AS observations
FROM analytics.fact_food_prices f
JOIN analytics.dim_commodity c ON f.commodity_id = c.commodity_id
JOIN analytics.dim_date d ON f.date_id = d.date_id
WHERE c.commodity_name = 'Maize (white)' AND d.year = 2020
GROUP BY d.year, f.price_type;

Result:
• Retail: 54.57 KES per KG (97 observations)
• Wholesale: 33.56 KES per KG (12 observations)
Finding: Confirms a 62.6% retail markup covering transport, packaging, and retail margins.

Query 2: Seasonality Impact on Staple Food Prices (Harvest vs Off-Season):
SQL:
SELECT c.commodity_name,
       CASE WHEN d.is_harvest_season THEN 'Harvest Season' ELSE 'Off-Season / Planting' END AS season,
       ROUND(AVG(f.price_per_kg)::numeric, 2) AS avg_price_per_kg
FROM analytics.fact_food_prices f
JOIN analytics.dim_commodity c ON f.commodity_id = c.commodity_id
JOIN analytics.dim_date d ON f.date_id = d.date_id
WHERE c.commodity_name IN ('Beans (dry)', 'Maize (white)', 'Potatoes (Irish)')
GROUP BY c.commodity_name, d.is_harvest_season
ORDER BY c.commodity_name, season;

Result:
• Beans (dry): 94.70 KES/KG in harvest vs 98.23 KES/KG in off-season (-3.6%)
• Maize (white): 39.72 KES/KG in harvest vs 40.82 KES/KG in off-season (-2.7%)
• Potatoes (Irish): 49.75 KES/KG in harvest vs 54.74 KES/KG in off-season (-9.1%)
Finding: Statistically confirms supply surge during Kenya's dual harvest windows reduces consumer market prices.

1. What I Built & How It Works:
For this milestone, I automated the monthly ETL workflow using Apache Airflow running on an Ubuntu production VPS. 

• DAG File: dags/kenya_food_prices_dag.py
• DAG ID: kenya_food_prices_etl_pipeline
• Schedule: Cron '0 6 15 * *' (Triggers at 06:00 AM on the 15th of every month, matching KNBS monthly market release cycles).
• Resilience Policy: Configured with retries=3, retry_delay=15s exponential backoff, and execution_timeout=15m.
• Task Pipeline:
  check_source_health >> extract_raw_data >> transform_clean_normalise >> load_postgres_warehouse >> validate_data_quality_and_audit
